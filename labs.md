[ML platform interactive labs](/labs/ml-platform.html): step through the PyTorch DataLoader loop, compare training with and without `DistributedSampler`, see where time goes with `persistent_workers`, and delete or crash Pods behind a Kubernetes Service.

### Flashcards

[ML platform flashcards](/labs/flashcards.html): tap-to-reveal cards on DDP, data loading, Kubernetes, and system design answers, written as *what / how / so what*.
